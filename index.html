Reading the Card

What every number, badge and icon on a FIFA-style card actually means.

Overall Rating (OVR)

The large number in the top-left corner. A single 1–99 score summarising how strong the player is overall. The position sits just below it (e.g. ST, CB, CM).

Rating Trend

The small arrow next to each name. Ratings are reviewed monthly against current form — a ▲ means the OVR went up since the last update, ▼ means it dropped, and ▬ means no change.

The Six Face Stats

The row along the bottom of an outfield card — the headline attributes that drive almost everything in-game.

PACPace — sprint speed and acceleration
SHOShooting — finishing, shot power, long shots
PASPassing — vision, crossing, set pieces
DRIDribbling — ball control, agility, balance
DEFDefending — tackling, marking, interceptions
PHYPhysical — strength, stamina, aggression

Goalkeeper Stats

Keepers get their own six stats instead of the outfield ones.

DIVDiving — reach and spring across the goal
HANHandling — holding and catching the ball
KICKicking — distribution range and accuracy
REFReflexes — reaction speed to close-range shots
SPDSpeed — sprint speed off the line
POSPositioning — reading the game and set position

Season Goals & Assists

Each card now shows the player's goals and assists for the current season, pulled from the Player Stats page. Updated after every game.

Alternate Positions

The stacked tags on the right edge (e.g. LB, CB). Extra positions the player can be played in without losing chemistry — useful for fitting the team together.

Preferred Foot & Weak Foot

The R / L badge shows the stronger foot. A player's weak-foot rating (1–5 stars) controls how accurate and powerful they are shooting or passing with the other foot.

PlayStyles & PlayStyles+

The small diamond icons down the left edge of the card. Signature abilities that change how a player performs specific actions — for example:

  • Finesse Shot / Power Shot — better curled or driven finishing
  • Incisive Pass / Pinged Pass — sharper, faster passing
  • Quick Step / Rapid — explosive acceleration
  • Jockey / Block / Anticipate — stronger defending
  • Power Header / Bruiser — physical dominance

A gold diamond (PlayStyles+) is the enhanced version — a noticeably bigger boost than the standard silver one.

Team Chemistry

A team-building score, not printed on the base card. Each player earns chemistry points (up to 3) when they share a club, league or nation with team-mates, for a squad total of 33. The more links a player has, the bigger the in-game boost to their attributes. Play players in their listed position to keep chemistry active.

Chemistry Style

A consumable applied to a card that re-distributes attribute upgrades across the six stats (it does not raise the OVR number). Common ones:

  • Hunter — +Pace, +Shooting
  • Shadow — +Pace, +Defending
  • Engine — +Pace, +Passing, +Dribbling
  • Sniper — +Shooting, +Dribbling
  • Anchor — +Pace, +Defending, +Physical
  • Glove / Cat — goalkeeper styles (+Diving/Handling or +Reflexes)

The boost scales with the player's chemistry — a style on 3 chemistry gives the full effect.
